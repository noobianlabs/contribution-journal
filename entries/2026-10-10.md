### Practical first step

Read [Toolbar.tsx](https://github.com/tiko-run/tiko/blob/main/frontend/src/canvas/Toolbar.tsx) and [JqlInput.tsx](https://github.com/tiko-run/tiko/blob/main/frontend/src/canvas/JqlInput.tsx). `AddCardForm` already resolves input through a mutation and calls `onAdd` only when `tasks.length > 0`; that is the success boundary for remembering the submitted input, including partial successes. Do not remember zero-result or failed inputs.

`JqlInput` already has a combobox, listbox, highlighted options, and arrow/Enter handling. Reuse that interaction rather than adding a second picker. History selection should restore the entire saved input; existing JQL token completion uses `applySuggestion`, so avoid accidentally treating history as a single token. Keep the newest five distinct successful inputs, handle malformed or unavailable browser storage gracefully, and preserve existing JQL completion and form submission.
